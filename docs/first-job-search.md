# Step 4: Run Your First Batch Job Search

1. Go to the [Commonwealth of Pennsylvania Job Opportunities page on GovernmentJobs.com](https://www.governmentjobs.com/careers/pabureau) (or start from [Employment.pa.gov](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/employment) and click **Open Jobs**).
2. Filter by your **Location / County** or **Department** on the left sidebar if desired.
3. Click your **`⚡ CWOPA Extractor`** bookmarklet on your browser's bookmarks bar.
4. In the **Batch Job Scanner** modal:
   * Set **Pages** to `1` for your first scan.
   * Under **Advanced Exclusions**, **uncheck** *"Exclude Non-Remote"* if you want to see in-person jobs in your area (it is checked by default for telework/remote searches).
5. Click **Scan Jobs**, then click **Copy Payload** once the results load, and paste the payload into your AI Career Co-Pilot chat!
