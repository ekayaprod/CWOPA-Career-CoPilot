# Step 6: Completing an Application & Using Questionnaire Auto-Fill

1. **Extract Questions:** On the NEOGOV `/apply/questions` step, click your **`⚡ CWOPA Extractor`** bookmarklet, click **Copy Payload**, and paste the `### APPLICATION QUESTIONS` into your AI chat.
2. **Review First Pass & Audit:** Read the drafted answers and answer the Co-Pilot's **Intercept Verification Questions** to confirm your exact scope, independence level, and facts.
3. **Request JSON Export:** Once verified, tell the AI: *"Please generate the JSON block for auto-fill."*
4. **Verify & Auto-Fill:** Click **`⚡ CWOPA Extractor`** on the `/apply/questions` page, click **Auto-Fill Answers**, paste the JSON block, click **Verify Payload** to inspect word counts and edits, and click **Approve & Inject**.
