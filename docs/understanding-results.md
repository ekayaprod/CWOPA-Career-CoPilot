# Step 5: Understanding Your Triage Results & Creative Pivots

When you paste a `### ENHANCED VIABILITY SCAN` payload into your AI Career Co-Pilot, it organizes the batch into four categories:

| Category | What It Means | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Direct Fits** | Your past job duties and experience directly satisfy the stated Minimum Experience and Training (MET) requirements. | High priority—inspect the full posting and apply! |
| **2. Creative Pivots** | The job title or state agency looks unfamiliar, **but the actual day-to-day work mechanics match skills you have already demonstrated.** | Read the Co-Pilot's plain-English bridge and grade (`Strong`, `Moderate`, or `Speculative`). |
| **3. Hard Gate Blockers** | The role requires a mandatory license, specific degree, internal civil-service status, or certification that cannot be substituted. | Skip unless you actually hold that credential. |
| **4. Pay or Level Gaps** | You qualify for the role, but the salary range or responsibility level is substantially below your target level. | Keep only as a backup option. |
