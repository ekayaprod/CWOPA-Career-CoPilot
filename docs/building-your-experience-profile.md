# Step 3: Build Your Experience Profile & NEOGOV Work History

Before the Co-Pilot can spot **Creative Pivots** or draft application answers, it needs to know what you have actually done.

## Three Ways to Start

1. **You Already Have a Resume:** Upload or paste your resume into your AI chat after loading the Master Prompt. The Co-Pilot will review your roles using its **7-Level Independence Scale** (*Observed -> Assisted -> Performed under supervision -> Performed independently -> Led -> Approved -> Designed*) and help you build an Evergreen NEOGOV Work History.
2. **You Already Have a NEOGOV Account Profile:** Open your NEOGOV Work Experience page (`/apply/work` or `/profile`), click your **`⚡ CWOPA Extractor`** bookmarklet, click **Copy Payload**, and paste it into your AI chat.
3. **You Don't Have a Resume Ready:** Tell the Co-Pilot *"I don't have a current resume ready—let's build my experience profile together"* and answer a few conversational questions about what your typical week looked like in your recent jobs.
