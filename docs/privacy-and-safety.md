# Privacy, Data Safety, and Hiring Process Disclaimers

* **100% Local Bookmarklet Execution:** `CWOPA-Neogov-Extractor.js` runs strictly in your browser on the pages you choose to scan. It has no external tracking servers and never accesses your NEOGOV password.
* **User-Controlled AI Sharing:** Information is only shared with ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude when you copy and paste a payload or upload a document. Review your chosen AI provider's privacy settings, and feel free to omit your street address or phone number when uploading a resume.
* **Commonwealth Screening Variations:** While the Co-Pilot models the standard Office of Administration / NEOGOV Minimum Experience and Training (MET) screening workflow, specific screening and interview procedures vary across classifications, union agreements, exam vs. non-exam tracks, and independent state commissions. See [Employment.pa.gov FAQs](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/employment/how-to-apply/faqs) for official guidance.
