Commonwealth Career Co-Pilot A practical helper for PA state job applications

Your past work fits more PA state jobs than you think.

State job postings often use unfamiliar titles that hide how well your day-to-day skills actually fit. This free two-part setup—a browser bookmarklet and an AI chat prompt—helps you scan openings in bulk, spot Creative Pivots outside your old industry, and write clear, honest applications.

3-Step Setup Guide

You only need to do Steps 1 and 2 once on your computer.

1

Add the Extractor Bookmarklet to Your Browser Bar

Works on desktop Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, and Safari.

⚡ CWOPA Extractor Drag Me Up!

Option A: Drag & Drop (Easiest)

  1. Make sure your browser's Bookmarks Bar is visible (Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows or Cmd+Shift+B on Mac).
  2. Click and hold the blue ⚡ CWOPA Extractor button above and drag it onto your Bookmarks Bar.
  3. Go to GovernmentJobs.com / PA Bureau and click your new bookmark!

Option B: Manual Copy-Paste Fallback

Copy the Master Prompt into Your AI Platform

Works in any regular chat or inside a persistent Project / Gem.

Once you paste the prompt into ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude, it will ask how you want to start:

You can upload a resume, describe your last job in a few plain sentences, or paste a batch of jobs from the bookmarklet.

3

What Page Should I Click the Bookmarklet On?

The bookmarklet automatically detects which NEOGOV page you are viewing and opens the matching tool:

1. Job Search List

Scans a whole page of job openings at once so the AI can spot your best matches.

2. Single Job Page

Copies the full job description, pay, and requirements for a closer look.

3. Work History Page

Grabs your saved NEOGOV work history so the AI can help you polish it.

4. Application Questions

Copies the application questions—and fills your finished answers back onto the page.

Use It With ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude

Paste the prompt into a regular chat for free, or save it in a Project or Gem so you don't have to paste it again next time.

ChatGPT

Quick start: Open chatgpt.com, paste the prompt into a new chat, and attach your resume.

Save for later: Put the prompt inside ChatGPT Projects along with your resume so it's ready whenever you return.

Google Gemini

Quick start: Open gemini.google.com, paste the prompt into a new chat, and upload your resume.

Save for later: Save the prompt inside a Gemini Gem (or Skill) and attach your resume file.

Anthropic Claude

Quick start: Open claude.ai, paste the prompt into a new chat, and upload your resume.

Save for later: Create a free Claude Project, add the prompt to Project Instructions, and upload your resume.
