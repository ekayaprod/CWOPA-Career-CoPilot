Platform-Neutral Architecture

Add the Co-Pilot to ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude

One master prompt works everywhere. Use Direct Paste on any free account, or set up a persistent workspace so your resume stays loaded.

ChatGPT (OpenAI)

Free & Plus

Direct Paste: Start a new chat at chatgpt.com, paste the Master Prompt, and attach your resume.

Persistent Setup (ChatGPT Projects): Create a Project named PA Career Co-Pilot, paste the Master Prompt into Project Instructions, and upload your Resume + Evidence Repository to Project Files.

Why not a Custom GPT? Personal/free OpenAI accounts cannot build custom GPTs, whereas Direct Paste and Projects work without builder restrictions.

Google Gemini

Free & Advanced

Direct Paste: Open gemini.google.com, paste the Master Prompt into a new conversation, and upload your resume via the + button.

Persistent Setup (Gems / Skills): Create a Gem or Skill named PA Career Co-Pilot, paste the Master Prompt into Instructions, and attach your Resume file.

Platform Note: Google is transitioning Gems to Skills beginning November 2026. The Master Prompt works identically in both.

Anthropic Claude

Free & Pro

Direct Paste: Open claude.ai, paste the Master Prompt into a new chat, and attach your resume.

Persistent Setup (Claude Projects): Click Projects → Create Project, paste the Master Prompt into Project Instructions, and add your Resume to Project Knowledge.

Tip: Claude Projects are available on free accounts (up to 5 projects) and excel at strict Rule 0 verification audits.
