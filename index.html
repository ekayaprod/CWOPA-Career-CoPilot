Computer · Optional

PA Jobs Bookmark

A browser bookmarklet that runs directly on governmentjobs.com with no extension to install:

  • Batch Scanner: Scans a page of search results at once and flags eligibility gates.
  • Question & History Grabber: Pulls tagged Q01..Qnn questions and saved NEOGOV work history.
  • Answer Checker & Auto-Fill: Checks character limits, required fields, and AI tells before filling the form.

Show your Bookmarks Bar (Ctrl+Shift+B or Cmd+Shift+B) and drag this button up to it:

PA Jobs Bookmark
Can't drag? Copy code for manual setup
  1. Click Copy PA Jobs Bookmark Code below.
  2. Right-click your Bookmarks Bar and choose Add page or Add bookmark.
  3. Name it PA Jobs Bookmark, paste the code into the URL / Address box, and save.

Before you start: Remove your street address and phone number before uploading a resume. The prompt tells the AI never to invent experience, and you confirm every fact.

How the Workflow Goes

  1. 1

    Load your background

    Paste the Job Helper Prompt into your AI chat and share your resume or work history to get tailored PA Job Category filters.

  2. 2

    Compare openings

    Filter the PA State Jobs Portal ↗ and paste postings into your chat so the AI can check minimum requirements and rank your best options. Computer shortcut: Click PA Jobs Bookmark on a search results page to scan the list at once.

  3. 3

    Build your pitch on the Job Questions

    Share the posting and its Job Questions (from the posting's Questions tab or the application step); the AI interviews you for concrete details from your past work to draft answers for the highest score you honestly qualify for. Computer shortcut: Click PA Jobs Bookmark on the posting or questions page to copy all questions at once.

  4. 4

    Check & fill the application

    Review your drafted answers for accuracy and paste them into the form. Computer shortcut: On the Questions page, click PA Jobs Bookmark → Auto-Fill Answers, paste the chat's auto-fill code, click Check Answers (Recommended), then click Approve & Fill Form.

Pitching Non-Linear Experience & "Creative Pivots"

You rarely have a straight-line title match when switching fields or moving across state departments. The helper breaks your past work into underlying mechanics to spot illustrative Direct Fits and Creative Pivots:

State Employee (Cross-Series Promotion) Surfaces above-class duties, SOP writing, onboarding, and queue tracking to bridge classification series. Targets: Management Analyst 1 · Administrative Officer 1 · Program Analyst
Private-Sector Office & Operations Maps corporate vendor quotes, budget tracking, and project coordination to Commonwealth MET rubrics. Targets: Administrative Officer 1 · Purchasing Agent · Management Technician
IT Support & Systems Administration Separates independent troubleshooting and scripting from assisted tasks to maximize technical KSA credit. Targets: IT Generalist 1 · Network Specialist 1 · Applications Developer
Medical Front Desk & Insurance Billing Translates patient intake, coverage verification, and denied-claim follow-up into state casework & revenue roles. Targets: Income Maintenance Caseworker · UC Claims Interviewer · Tax Collections Tech
Warehouse, Inventory & Dispatch Connects manifest verification, inventory software, and safety checks to state supply and inspection specs. Targets: Stock Clerk 3 · Purchasing Agent · Safety Inspector Trainee
Retail & Food-Service Supervision Turns crew scheduling, register balancing, and delivery invoice checks into administrative & procurement credit. Targets: Purchasing Agent · Administrative Officer 1 · Liquor Store Manager 1A
